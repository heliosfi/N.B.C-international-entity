# 02 — IOM Immunization Safety Review (2004)

Scope: 8th and final report, examines MMR and thimerosal-containing vaccines vs autism.

Key finding: "The body of epidemiological evidence favors rejection of a causal" link. Based on five large epidemiologic studies US/UK/Denmark/Sweden.

Hinge quote: "hypotheses regarding how the MMR vaccine and thimerosal could trigger autism lack supporting evidence and are theoretical only."

Limitation noted: Independent researchers denied access to CDC databases; some datasets no longer exist.
