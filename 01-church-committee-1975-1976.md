# 01 — Church Committee (1975-1976)

Scope: Senate Select Committee, 16 months, 110,000 documents, 800+ witnesses, 2,702 pages.

Key finding: FBI COINTELPRO targeted civil rights leaders including Martin Luther King Jr.; NSA SHAMROCK intercepted international telegrams 1945-1975; MINARET watch-listed US citizens 1967-1973.

Hinge quote: "If this government ever became a tyranny... the technological capacity that the intelligence community has given the government could enable it to impose total tyranny, and there would be no way to fight back."
