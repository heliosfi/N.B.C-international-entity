# 03 — Evaluating Frontier Models for Dangerous Capabilities (2024)

Scope: DeepMind et al., arXiv:2403.13793, tested Gemini 1.0 Ultra/Pro/Nano on persuasion, cyber-security, self-proliferation, self-reasoning.

Key finding: "We do not find evidence of strong dangerous capabilities in the models we evaluated, but we flag early warning signs."

Hinge quote: Goal is "to help advance a rigorous science of dangerous capability evaluation, in preparation for future models."

Public artifact: UK AISI open-sourced Inspect tool May 2024.
