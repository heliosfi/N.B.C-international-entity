# 05 — House Oversight Surveillance Pricing (2026)

Scope: Chairman James Comer investigation opened March 2026, FTC briefing Sep 2026.

Key finding: "consumers are frequently unable to determine whether or how their personal data is being used to determine the price they are charged"

Hinge quote: Companies create profiles from "geolocation, demographics, browsing history, purchase history, device type, battery life, and mouse clicks to assign different prices to different individuals in ways consumers cannot understand, anticipate, or control."

Context: FTC 6(b) study Jan 2025 documented data broker practices.
